# 0011 — Form-sourced album review submissions are a separate archive, not the ADR 0006 Review model

WXYC's ~1,650 DJ-written album reviews collected since March 2021 through the "Album Review Responses" Google Form live in their own table, [`album_review_submissions`](../../shared/database/src/schema.ts) (migration 0119), synced nightly by [`jobs/album-reviews-etl/`](../../jobs/album-reviews-etl/). They do NOT extend the existing `reviews` table, which [ADR 0006](0006-reviews-model-extension.md) (canonical cross-repo ADR 0005 in the iOS DJ-tool repo) reserves as the one-per-album, author-owned (`author_dj_id` FK to `auth_user`), MD-queued in-app Review model with CRUD at `/reviews`.

The two review concepts conflict on every structural axis, which is why merging them was rejected (decision 2026-07-16, user-confirmed, after a first draft that evolved `reviews` was invalidated in review):

- **Cardinality**: the form archive holds multiple independent reviews per album (14 (artist, album) pairs have 2–3 today); ADR 0006's `reviews` is at most one per album (`album_id` UNIQUE).
- **Authorship**: form reviewers are free-text strings — many are alumni with no `auth_user` row and never will have one; ADR 0006 reviews are owned by a real account with edit/transfer rules.
- **Mutability**: form submissions are an append-only archive curated only via the sheet (the ETL UPSERTs content edits but never deletes and never reassigns identity); ADR 0006 reviews are living documents with author edits, MD transfer, and a claim queue.
- **Identity**: a submission's identity is free-text `(artist_name, album_title)` plus its `source_key`; `album_id` is a best-effort, never-overwritten singleton link that survives library deletions (FK ON DELETE SET NULL). ADR 0006 reviews are library-bound by construction.

## Decision

- `album_review_submissions` is the form archive: append-only, multi-per-album, free-text identity, PII-internal reviewer fields (`reviewer_raw`, `social_consent_raw`) that no read endpoint ever emits (the `flowsheet.dj_name` posture — the form promised "your name will not be shared").
- **There are two distinct read surfaces over this archive, with deliberately different consent postures. They must not be conflated, and neither may inherit the other's posture by copy-paste.**
  - **The consent-gated per-album attach — built, and the only surface that exists today.** `GET /proxy/metadata/album` attaches a `wxycReviews` array (schema `WxycReviewItem`, wxyc-shared#259) for the album the request already resolved, served by `lookupWxycReviewsByAlbumId` in [`album-metadata-lookup.service.ts`](../../apps/backend/services/album-metadata-lookup.service.ts) and gated by the `WXYC_REVIEWS_ENABLED` env flag (default off). This is a **publishing** surface aimed at DJ- and listener-facing apps, so consent is a hard filter enforced in SQL — `social_consent = true`, never `IS NOT FALSE` and never `IS NOT NULL`, because `social_consent` is NULL whenever the ETL could not parse the reviewer's answer and an unparsed answer is not consent. The reviewer-identity columns are excluded by the query's explicit column list rather than by a downstream mapper.
  - **The browse endpoint — designed but never built.** `GET /album-reviews` (with the resource-named `album_reviews` response array) was specified as an internal/curatorial browse over the whole archive, returning every row with `social_consent` as a decorative field rather than a filter. It was prototyped in the **closed** PR #1679 (branch `feature/album-reviews-endpoint`) and its path was subsequently removed from `api.yaml` in the phantom-path sweep. No such endpoint is deployed.
  - **The cross-link that matters:** the browse posture is safe only for a curatorial surface. If `/album-reviews` is ever revived, it MUST NOT be wired to a public or app-facing client without adopting the attach's hard `social_consent = true` gate and the same reviewer-identity exclusion. Reviving it as specified and pointing an app at it would publish non-consented review bodies — the exact outcome the form's "your name will not be shared" promise and the consent question were collected to prevent.
- The `/reviews` namespace, the `reviews` table, ADR 0006, and `tests/integration/fk-on-delete-flowsheet-rotation-reviews.spec.js` stay untouched and reserved for the in-app Review model.
- Neither model migrates into the other. A future in-app Review MAY cite a submission row as provenance (e.g. a `submission_id` reference added by the ADR 0006 implementation), but that is a citation, not a merge.

## Consequences

- Later work touching "reviews" must first decide which of the two concepts it means; this ADR is the boundary marker that prevents the archive from being folded into the in-app model (or vice versa) by well-meaning consolidation.
- **Consent is a row-level property enforced in SQL, never a deployment-level one.** `WXYC_REVIEWS_ENABLED` decides whether the surface is served at all; `social_consent = true` decides which rows may appear on it. Flipping the flag on can only widen the audience for rows that already cleared consent — it can never promote a non-consented row. Any future read path over this table inherits that split: the flag is optional, the SQL predicate is not.
- **The archive's reach through the consented attach is a small fraction of the archive.** Three gates compose — consented, linked to a `library` album, and non-empty body — and as of 2026-08-25 they leave 252 of 1,689 rows (744 linked; 656 consented). A reader comparing "1,650 reviews" against what the app shows should expect that gap and not read it as a bug. Raising the _linked_ share is `jobs/album-reviews-etl/`'s concern, not the serve path's.
- **The attach mirrors the external critic-reviews mechanism (ADR 0012) structurally but not editorially.** Critic reviews are excerpt-capped with a mandatory link-out because they are third-party copyrighted text; DJ reviews are WXYC-authored, so the full body is surfaced. The binding constraint here is consent and reviewer anonymity, not copyright — which is why the two keep separate flags, separate tables, and separate wire fields.
- **Reaching the listener app's playcut-detail view would need a contract change, and is not what the attach above does.** `GET /proxy/metadata/album` is called unconditionally by the DJ-facing catalog surfaces (`wxyc-dj-ios`, `wxyc-dj-android`, dj-site), but `wxyc-ios-64` skips it for rows whose `metadata_status` is terminal — the short-circuit that made external critic reviews unreachable there until [BS#1869](https://github.com/WXYC/Backend-Service/issues/1869) inlined `critic_reviews` into the V2 flowsheet feed. Surfacing DJ reviews on that same playcut path would likewise require a new `wxyc_reviews` field on `FlowsheetV2TrackEntry` in wxyc-shared plus a feed-assembly attach; that is a separate, deliberate decision, not an automatic mirror of #1869.
- The archive's writer is exactly one job (`jobs/album-reviews-etl/`); its link pass is the only writer of `album_review_submissions.album_id`, and manual corrections always win over it.
- The dormant `feature/library-code-lookup-reviews` branch (PR #212, closed stale 2026-05-09) targets ADR 0006's `reviews` and is unaffected.
